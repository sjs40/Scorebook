Thursday night

Thursday, September 10

Anthony Volpe returned with a grand slam and five RBIs in a six-run seventh as the Yankees swept Colorado 10–3. Cam Smith's ninth-inning homer off Jhoan Duran lifted Houston past Philadelphia 2–1.
